Why this can work

Frequently asked questions

What is In-District?
A new resource now being shaped with California superintendents and business officials. In-District is a temporary name, and the services are not yet set.
Who can use this site?
California superintendents and chief business officials. The site checks your district email address against the California Department of Education School Directory.
How do you confirm who I am?
You enter your district email address and confirm by a code texted to your cell phone.
Will my name be shared?
Your name, email address and phone number will not be shared under any circumstances.
Would this replace my current association?
That is not decided. One of our questions asks whether a new resource should replace your current one or work alongside it.
What will it cost?
There is no price yet. How you would prefer to pay is one of the questions we ask.
Who is behind it?
Brian R. Demsey, a former Capistrano Unified School District trustee and the founder of an actuarial firm that served more than 400 California public sector entities.
